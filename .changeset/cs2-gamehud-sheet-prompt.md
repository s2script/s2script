`GameHud.sheet(player, spec)` opens a full panel with side tabs (grouped, collapsing and scrolling when they do not fit) and a paged body: a numbered list with stable numbers (open on rule 3 of a section, highlighted), paragraphs, toggles, a card grid or a table with row buttons. `GameHud.prompt(player, spec)` asks one player a question with up to four clickable answers, an optional timer and a Close button, and falls back to typed numbers in chat when the HUD refuses it.
