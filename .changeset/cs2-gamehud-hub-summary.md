`GameHud.intro(player, spec)` shows the mode's brand, a menu and a profile card on join. `GameHud.spectate(player, spec)` shows a spectate hub with a menu, a player picker and a camera shot list (follow shots and fixed shots through the player's own observer camera, optionally advancing on a timer). `GameHud.summary(audience, spec)` shows a passive round-end summary with headline stats and a short table.
