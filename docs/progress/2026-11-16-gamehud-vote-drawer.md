**GameHud vote and drawer (2026-11-16). Unit-tested, not live-tested.** Fourth slice of the gamemode HUD work. `games/cs2/js/gamekit-panels.js` drives `s2script_gamepanels.xml` and adds two calls to `GameHud`. `vote(spec)` pops up 2 to 8 options for a voter audience (viewers may be wider), claims `gamekit:vote` host-side so one vote runs at a time, and follows the input rule: it never grabs the cursor, voters type `1`-`8` in chat (the line is swallowed through `__s2_chat_on_message`) or press Tab (`HudInput.arm`) to click. Counts appear once you have voted; a timer meter counts down, and `onEnd` gets `{counts, winner, votes}` with `winner: null` on a tie. `drawer(player, spec)` is per player (`gamekit:drawer:<slot>`), takes the cursor, and paints up to 5 tabs and 8 rows (image, tone, sub, meta, selected, disabled) with a Close button (`gk_dr_close`, new in the layout, so the workshop addon needs republishing). Refused paints go to chat once. Live check: start a vote from the cookbook mid-round, type `2` in chat (it must not show), press Tab and click another option, then open a drawer and close it.
