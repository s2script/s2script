**Quieter slow-frame line (2026-11-18). Unit-tested; prompted by the first live run.** On a live server the slow-frame line from the watchdog printed about once a second at the 4 ms default, with a doubled `[s2script] [s2script]` prefix. The default threshold is now 8 ms (half a 64-tick frame, the share that can push a frame past the engine's own warning), the first slow frame is printed at once, and after that at most one line a minute describes the longest slow frame of that minute with a count. The line, and the map-start collection line, no longer add their own prefix, since the host logger does. `sm frames <ms>` still lowers the threshold for investigation.
