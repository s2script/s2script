**CI speed-up (2026-11-12).** Prompted by Gabriel calling the PR wait unacceptable: ci-native took about 7.5 minutes on Blacksmith (and 9 to 16 on ubuntu-latest before #362), almost all of it one serial chain of about 60 gates on a 4-vCPU runner where most gates are single-threaded ASan compiles. `scripts/ci-native.sh` is now four shards that share no build outputs (`rust`: boundary/license gates, cargo build/test, host shim; `fixtures`: the standalone C++/Python fixture gates; `engine-function`: the ABI build and everything reading it, including the V8 adapter diagnostics; `runtime`: the Bullseye Docker build and acceptance bundle), and `ci-native.yml` runs them as a matrix. A bare `ci-native.sh` still runs every shard, so local green still means CI green, and the rust shard fails if the workflow's matrix stops listing every shard. rust and engine-function share the original rust-cache entry (only rust saves it); runtime keeps its own because its Docker build fills `target/release` with Bullseye-compiled deps. In ci-js, `check-plugins-typecheck.sh` now typechecks its 52 targets in parallel with ordered output (38s to 19s on 4 cores).
