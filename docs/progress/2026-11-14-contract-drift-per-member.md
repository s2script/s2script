The loader no longer refuses on that hash (the batch fixed-point in `resolve_contract_rejections` and the apply-time check are gone). It logs one `contract drift on '<iface>'` warning when the consumer is about to start, naming the members the consumer compiled against that the producer has removed or retyped, or saying none changed. Protocol 2 contracts are compared member by member (`interop::Contract::method_drift`/`forward_drift`, exact schema equality): a call to a removed method throws `InterfaceUnknownMethod: <iface>.<method>`, a retyped one `InterfaceTypesMismatch: <iface>.<method>`, the same for `on(<forward>)` with `InterfaceUnknownForward`, and forward delivery skips only a consumer whose copy of that forward differs. The facade binds each unchanged method to the producer's function and each drifted one to its error, so the rest of a drifted interface keeps its fast path. Protocol 1 imports check only that the method exists (`CallTarget::UnknownMethod`).
