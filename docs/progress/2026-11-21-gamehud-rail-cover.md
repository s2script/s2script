**GameHud rail covers the stock team counter (2026-11-21). Unit-tested, not live-tested.** No `m_iHideHUD` bit hides CS2's stock team counter and round timer, so the GameHud rail floated over them with the stock avatars and timer showing around its score blocks. The rail is now, by default, a solid bar across the full width of the top edge, 116 px tall at 1080p, in the kit's opaque panel colour, with the score blocks, pips, clock and sub line inside it (`scripts/gamehud/base.css`). The size comes from CS2's own HUD styles: the counter is centred, starts about 6 px down, is about 106 px tall in a round and grows about 56 px per player on each side, with no aspect-ratio rules, so only the full width is sure to cover it on a big server. The bar overlaps the top of the stock radar and the first stock kill-feed row, and while a player is dead or spectating the stock equipment row (to about 182 px) still shows below it. `rail.set(audience, { cover: false })` keeps the floating look per player (a `gk-float` class on `gk_rail`); a new owner of the rail drops its diff cache for the rail and starts everyone on the cover. Cover is the default because the only caller, the cookbook recipe, paints team counts that duplicate the stock counter. The cookbook gained `sm_gk_rail [float|cover]`, and `docs/GAMEHUD.md` a Top rail section and a screenshot check at 16:9 and 4:3.
