**GameHud intro, spectate hub and round-end summary (2026-11-16). Unit-tested, not live-tested.** Sixth slice of the gamemode HUD work. `games/cs2/js/gamekit-hub.js` drives the hub panels with two separate calls that share one per-player claim (`gamekit:hub:<slot>`), so opening one replaces the other without a close event. `GameHud.intro(player, spec)` paints the brand, a menu of up to 8 rows and an optional profile card, and takes the cursor. `GameHud.spectate(player, spec)` adds a 12-card player picker and a camera shot list. A follow shot or a picker click runs `spec_mode 2|3` + `spec_player <slot+1>` through `Client.fakeCommand`; a fixed shot runs `spec_mode 4` + `spec_goto x y z pitch yaw`. `shotSeconds` advances the list on a timer, and picking a player stops it. The spectate hub takes the cursor unless `cursor: false`, which leaves the mouse to the camera and arms Tab. Both menus have a Close button (`gk_hub_list_close`, new in the layout). `GameHud.summary(audience, spec)` lives in `gamekit.js` on the in-round layout (new `gk_sum` panel: eyebrow, toned title and top edge, 4 stats, a 4x5 table), is shared like the toast, and falls back to one chat line.
