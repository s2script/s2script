**GameHud cookbook demo and live-check guide (2026-11-16). Typechecked, not live-tested.** Last slice of the gamemode HUD work. `examples/cookbook/src/recipes/gamehud.ts` uses every GameHud surface in one small jail-style mode: `sm_gk_on` claims the rail, status and feed and keeps them current from `player_death`, `player_hurt`, `player_spawn`, `round_start` and `round_end`, with a round-end summary; `sm_gk_vote`, `sm_gk_shop` (drawer with credits), `sm_rules [target] [section] [n]` (sheet read from `configs/gamehud-rules.json`, written on first use, opening on the viewer's team section), `sm_gk_ask` (timed prompt) and `sm_gk_intro` (intro to spectate hub to team pick and back). `docs/GAMEHUD.md` is the reference: the surface table with cursor and claim rules, the input rule, colours, chat fallback, stable rule numbers, camera shots, and a ten-step live check.
