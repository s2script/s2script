**GameHud weapon card (2026-11-18). Unit-tested, not live-tested.** A gamemode that hides the stock weapons HUD (`Hud.hide(slot, HideHud.Weapons)`, m_iHideHUD bit 6) used to lose the ammo count with it; TTT was the first to hit this. `s2script_gamehud.xml` gains a `gk_wpn` region bottom right (clear of chat on the left and the kill feed top right), in the status card's panel style: a five-row slot list (primary, secondary, knife, grenades, other; the active row takes the theme's accent edge), the weapon name with an optional `×N` count, and clip / reserve with the clip red at or under 25%. `games/cs2/js/gamekit-weapon.js` drives it as `GameHud.weapon`: `claim()` uses the same host claim as the rail and status card (`gamekit:weapon`) but answers a boolean, and `auto(audience, { name, hideSlots })` reads m_hActiveWeapon, m_iClip1 and m_pReserveAmmo[0] every frame and m_hMyWeapons every 8 frames (and on every switch), diffing against the last paint so a quiet frame sends nothing. Grenade counts come from the owner's `m_iAmmo[primaryAmmoType]` (new `WeaponData.primaryAmmoType`); names and slots come from a CS2 item-definition table, falling back to the VData gear slot. The card hides while the player is dead or spectating and follows a bot the player has taken over. Release and unload drop the frame subscription. `HideHud.Chat` was a wrong label for bit 6: it is now `HideHud.Weapons`, with `Chat` kept as a deprecated alias. The cookbook gains `sm_gk_weapon [off]`. The workshop addon must be republished for the new ids. Live check: `docs/GAMEHUD.md` step 10.
