**GameHud runtime (2026-11-15). Unit-tested, not live-tested.** Third slice of the gamemode HUD work. `games/cs2/js/gamekit.js` drives `s2script_gamehud.xml` from the ids in `gamekit.generated.js` and exposes `GameHud` from `@s2script/cs2`: `rail()`, `status()` and `feed()` each claim one region host-side (`__s2_ui_pool_claim("gamekit:<region>", 1)`), so two plugins cannot paint the same region and an unloaded owner's claim frees itself; `toast()` and `theme()` are shared. Every paint takes an audience (a player, a slot, a list, a filter or `"all"`). Text takes the chat colour tags: a leading tag picks the label's tone class, then the tags are stripped. When a kill-feed row or toast is refused for a live player, the same content goes to that player in chat; the rail and status card have no fallback. The layout entity is created on first use, not at plugin load. Live check: claim the rail and feed from the cookbook, kill a bot, and confirm the row appears only for its audience and fades after six seconds.
